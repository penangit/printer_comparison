Key point on "warranty": Neither quote mentions a standalone warranty because these are managed rental agreements — the vendor owns the equipment and is responsible for all repairs, parts, and consumables for the entire 60-month term. If a machine breaks, they fix or replace it at their cost. This is different from buying a copier outright where warranty matters. The real question is service response time — how fast does each vendor respond when something goes wrong? LBS has a track record with you; Dynacom is unproven but offering a 2-month demo to prove themselves.

Optional add-ons for the Dynacom Canon iR-ADV C3530i. Dynacom has confirmed these are only available if the reconditioned unit already has them fitted — they cannot be added afterwards. Ask Dynacom whether the demo/allocated units include either of these.

Single-Pass DADF-A1 if fitted

What it does: Replaces the standard DADF-AV1 with a single-pass duplex scanner that scans both sides of the page simultaneously — no flipping.
ADF capacity: 150 sheets (vs 100 on standard DADF-AV1)
Scan speed (1-sided): 80 ipm (vs 70 on standard)
Scan speed (2-sided): 160 ipm (vs 35 on standard — 4.6× faster) major upgrade
Why it matters: This single add-on transforms the Canon's biggest weakness. At 160 ipm duplex, it's faster than the HP E785 (120 ipm) and approaches your Sales Office E77660 (240 ipm). The 150-sheet tray also partially closes the gap from your current 200-sheet ADF. If you go Dynacom, insist on units with this fitted.

Inner Finisher-K1 if fitted

What it does: Adds stapling and offset sorting to the output tray.
Tray capacity: 2-tray, 600 sheets total (Tray A: 500 sheets, Tray B: 100 sheets)
Stapling: Corner or double staple, up to 50 sheets per set
Offset sorting: Yes (staggered output for collated sets)
Why it matters: Useful if your teams regularly produce multi-page stapled documents (reports, invoices, meeting packs). Without this, staff need to manually staple — a minor inconvenience at your volume but noticeable in Finance where collated reports are common. Neither your current machines nor the LBS E785 include a finisher by default either, so this would be a gain.
Key question for Dynacom: When you arrange the 2-month demo, ask specifically: "Do the units you're providing come with the Single-Pass DADF-A1 and/or Inner Finisher-K1?" If they can source units with the DADF-A1, the Canon becomes a much stronger contender — especially for the Sales Office where duplex scan speed matters most. Without it, the Sales Office feels the biggest downgrade.
Recommended Next Steps
You have roughly 2 weeks. Here's a suggested action plan.
  1. Take the Dynacom 2-month demo immediately. This is free and risk-free. Request a unit with the Single-Pass DADF-A1 if possible. Put it in the sales office for real-world testing alongside your existing HP.
  2. Ask Dynacom for the meter count on the recond units. A reconditioned copier with 50K pages on it is very different from one with 500K. This is the single biggest risk factor — get the numbers.
  3. Push Dynacom for C3100 pricing and timeline. If they can quote the imageFORCE C3130 (brand new, 30 ppm) within your decision window, it may be the best of both worlds — Canon pricing with new-equipment reliability.
  4. Use the Dynacom quote to negotiate LBS down. Show them the RM 235/month figure. Their RM 380 rental is hard to justify at your usage level. Ask for either a lower rental or 10K free copies instead of 5K.
  5. Clarify LBS E785 speed. The base spec is only 23 ppm — less than your current 40 ppm. Ask if the 28 ppm speed licence (8EP54AAE) is included, and whether a faster model is available.
  6. Decide and sign. Both Dynacom quotes have 7-day validity (expired 31 Aug). Get refreshed quotes and lock in before the vendor withdraws terms.
